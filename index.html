Model: one transverse quantum wavefunction ψ(y), propagated unitarily using a spectral Schrödinger solver with reflecting top/bottom boundaries. Obstacles impose position-dependent phase operations. The ball's horizontal travel is classical. Paddle detection samples position using the Born rule; a successful detection prepares a new localized outgoing packet. This is a quantum-inspired game, not a full simulation of quantum particle scattering in two spatial dimensions.
